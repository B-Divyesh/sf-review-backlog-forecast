01

Import queue totals

Import card due dates or a one-row summary. The file is read in this browser and never uploaded.

Accepted CSV columns

Summary: overdue,due_today,daily_due

Card rows: due_date (YYYY-MM-DD) or days_overdue, plus optional count.

Make a queue CSV from Anki
  1. In Anki Desktop, open Browse and search is:review prop:due<0.
  2. Copy the shown count into overdue in the downloaded template.
  3. Search is:review prop:due=0 and copy its count into due_today.
  4. Enter a usual day’s regular reviews in daily_due, then import the saved CSV.

Forecast assumptions

What moves cards in this forecast

  1. Today stays in the plan. Due-today and estimated regular reviews get capacity before overdue cards.
  2. One pace for every card. Minutes are card count × your seconds-per-review estimate.
  3. Rest days add regular reviews. Unreviewed regular reviews roll forward and stay visible.
  4. Anki scheduling is not simulated. Future intervals, retention, lapses, and FSRS changes remain unknown.
