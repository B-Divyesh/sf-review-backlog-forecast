What moves cards in this forecast

  1. Today stays in the plan. Due-today and estimated normal reviews get capacity before overdue cards.
  2. One pace for every card. Minutes are card count × your seconds-per-review estimate.
  3. Rest days add regular work. Unreviewed normal cards roll forward and stay visible.
  4. Anki scheduling is not simulated. Future intervals, retention, lapses, and FSRS changes remain unknown.
