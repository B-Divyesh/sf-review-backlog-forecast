Terms · Effective August 27, 2026

A forecast, not a scheduling command.

This free utility estimates review counts and time. It does not modify an Anki collection and cannot guarantee retention, learning outcomes, or future review intervals.

Use the plan as a planning aid. Check it against your energy, actual review pace, and the queue shown by your spaced-repetition app.

What the forecast includes

The simulation uses the counts and assumptions you provide, protects regular due work before overdue recovery, applies one average review time, and enforces your session cap.

What it cannot know

The forecast does not model answer ratings, lapses, retention, learning steps, future FSRS or SM-2 intervals, buried or suspended cards, timezone changes, or collection configuration. Actual queues will differ.
