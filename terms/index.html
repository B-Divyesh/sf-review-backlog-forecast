Use forecasts as planning estimates.

This free utility estimates review counts and time. It does not modify an Anki collection and cannot guarantee retention, learning outcomes, or future review intervals.

Use the plan as a planning aid. Check it against your energy, actual review pace, and the queue shown by your spaced-repetition app.

What the forecast includes

The simulation uses your counts and assumptions. It protects regular reviews before it schedules the overdue queue. It applies one average review time and enforces your session cap.

What it cannot know

The forecast does not model answer ratings, lapses, retention, learning steps, or future scheduler changes. It also excludes buried cards, suspended cards, timezone changes, and collection settings. Actual queues will differ.
